Places You'll Visit

Namugongo Martyrs Shrine

The most important Catholic shrine in Uganda, built on the site where St Charles Lwanga and fellow martyrs were executed in 1886 for their faith. Visit the Martyrs Museum and learn about the heroic witness of the Uganda Martyrs.

Munyonyo Martyrs Shrine

The place where the persecution of Uganda's first Christian martyrs began. Located near the former palace grounds of King Mwanga II.

Rubaga Cathedral

The headquarters of the Catholic Church in Uganda with beautiful architecture and rich Catholic history.

St Matia Mulumba Church

Dedicated to one of Uganda's most revered martyrs who gave his life for the Christian faith.

Our Lady of Kibeho Shrine

Africa's most famous Marian pilgrimage site where apparitions of Our Lady were reported beginning in 1981. The shrine includes the Apparition Square, Church of Sorrows, Marian Spring and the Rosary of the Seven Sorrows pathway.

Feast Day Celebrations

Participate in solemn Masses, candlelit processions, rosaries, spiritual reflections and celebrations marking the anniversary of Our Lady's first apparition at Kibeho.

Places You'll Visit

St Peter's Basilica

Built on the site where St Peter was martyred and buried. One of Christianity's most sacred basilicas.

Papal Audience

Experience a General Audience in St Peter's Square and receive the blessing of Pope Leo XIV, subject to the papal schedule.

Assisi

Visit the Basilica of St Francis, home to the saint's tomb, Giotto frescoes and the Basilica of St Clare with her relics and the miraculous crucifix.

Loreto Holy House

One of Italy's greatest pilgrimage sites which preserves the Holy House traditionally associated with the Blessed Virgin Mary.

Medjugorje

A world-famous Marian pilgrimage destination known for prayer, conversion, Eucharistic adoration, Apparition Hill and Mount Krisevac.

Mostar

Explore the famous Stari Most bridge and experience the rich blend of Christian and Muslim heritage.

Dubrovnik

Discover Croatia's stunning Adriatic coast and historic old city walls known throughout the world.
